Little things. Wonderful places. Look closer.

FindIt: Hidden Objects Game is a colorful offline hidden-object adventure. Explore scenes, search for the items on your checklist, and discover the small details hiding in plain sight. Take your time with a relaxed search or test your observation skills against the clock.

Explore 60 offline levels
Enjoy 60 bundled levels without an internet connection. Pan around each scene and zoom in to get a closer look at hidden items.

Play your way
Choose relaxed gameplay for an unhurried search, or try timed gameplay when you want an extra challenge. Follow the object checklist to see what you still need to find.

Hints, coins, and stars
Earn coins, build your score, and collect stars as you play. When an item is hard to spot, two-stage hints can help guide your search.

Daily Challenges
Take on Daily Challenges and earn bonus rewards for finding hidden objects.

Pick up where you left off
Save and resume your progress, with game data stored locally on your device. Adjust sound, music, and vibration to make each session comfortable for you.

Simple and private
No accounts, ads, analytics, or in-app purchases. Your progress and settings stay on your device, and the complete privacy policy is available offline in the app.
