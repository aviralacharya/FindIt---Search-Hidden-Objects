FindIt - Search Hidden Objects asset provenance

The in-app vector glyphs, vector brand illustrations, classic scene backgrounds, synthesized audio,
fonts, and 57 classic hidden-object compositions are reused from the local
FindIt project at E:\Google Apps\Find It. The original project is not modified.
verification/source-reuse.json records copied paths and original SHA-256 hashes.

The Android launcher/splash, web icons/splash, and Play listing icon now use the
publisher-supplied green magnifying-glass/star/leaf PNG, not the original app's
launcher artwork. The untouched original is assets/branding/icon_source.png:
SHA-256 a861fa79eacf1ba4f31601d54d4b37384b4a3c8827b55b4e1beaa1aff2e668e1.
verification/branding/icon-source.json records its dimensions and import provenance.
tool/prepare_branding_test.dart generates the color assets and padded Android
safe-zone foregrounds; it preserves enclosed highlights while removing only
edge-connected background for adaptive/splash assets. The Android themed icon
uses a simplified magnifier/star/leaf silhouette. verification/branding/manifest.json
records every generated file, checksum, dimension, and adaptive safe-zone check.
The supplied image's creation method, ownership, and distribution rights were
not independently established. The publisher must approve these before release.

The new Home, Garden, and Cafe compositions are authored in
lib/core/content/featured_scenes.dart and lib/core/art/illustrated_scene_art.dart.
Their three PNGs are exported by tool/export_hidden_art_test.dart, which also
checks that every featured target has visible pixels within its hit region.
They do not contain copied commercial characters, stock photos, or platform emoji.
The exported PNGs are deterministic rasterizations of those editable sources.
The reused sound effects and garden_loop.wav were synthesized in the original
project; they do not sample third-party recordings. No new audio was downloaded.

Fonts are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See the separate OFL files.
SQLite is public domain; Drift and its worker are MIT-licensed.
Dependencies may have their own licenses; Flutter's license registry is accessible
from Settings > About > Open-source licenses.

This provenance record is not a legal opinion or an exclusivity/trademark claim.
Before distribution, review the FindIt name, artwork quality, source provenance,
and rights with the publisher. This app uses colorful illustrated vector art,
not photography or photorealistic imagery. No external art service was used to
generate the scene PNGs or prepare the supplied icon in this project.
