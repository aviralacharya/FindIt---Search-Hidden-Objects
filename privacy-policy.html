Privacy Policy

FindIt

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Policy version 1.0.0

FindIt is a hidden-object game developed by Abiral Acharya. Its included puzzles work offline. The app does not require an account, show advertising, or send your gameplay data to the developer.

No accountLocal game savesNo ads or analytics

1. Which app this policy covers

This policy covers FindIt, the Android hidden-object game developed by Abiral Acharya.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally so you can continue playing. Progress is stored in the app’s local SQLite database. Settings and a flag recording whether you have started playing are stored in app preferences. This information is used only for gameplay, reliable saving, and your chosen settings; the app does not upload your saves or scores. There is no account-based recovery, cloud sync, or developer-hosted save service.

The settings format also preserves unused compatibility fields for age group and advertising. This app does not ask for or determine your age, use those fields to profile you, or enable advertising. Fresh installations use the unknown age-group value and advertising remains disabled.

  • Level progress, completed puzzles, scores, stars, personal bests, elapsed play time, accuracy, combos, play counts, and completion dates.
  • Earned coin balances and transactions, including their amounts, reasons, and timestamps. Coins are game rewards, not money or purchases.
  • Hints and reveal stages, found objects, the current puzzle, and daily-challenge dates, completion history, and bonus status.
  • Local puzzle/session identifiers and completion receipts used to resume play and prevent duplicate rewards. These are gameplay records, not advertising IDs or device identifiers.
  • Sound, music, vibration, volume, and relaxed or timed gameplay settings.
  • Locally installed puzzle content, if any.

3. Collection, use, and sharing

The app contains no advertising, advertising-consent, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not use an advertising ID, App Set ID, or persistent device identifier for tracking. It does not sell personal information or share gameplay data with advertisers or other companies. It does not ask for your name, date of birth, email address, location, or an account to play. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

If you choose to email the developer for support or a privacy request, your email provider and the developer receive the information you send, including your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. It is used to respond to your request, investigate an issue you report, and handle necessary legal obligations, not for advertising. Please do not include passwords, payment details, a child’s personal information, or unnecessary personal information.

The developer’s support inbox uses Google/Gmail, which processes email as a service provider. Support information is not sold or shared for advertising; it is handled by the developer and the email services needed to deliver, store, and respond to your message. Information may be disclosed only when necessary to comply with a valid legal requirement. External providers’ own privacy policies also apply.

When you tap Copy developer email, the app writes the published developer email address to the Android clipboard. It does not automatically read clipboard contents, copy gameplay records, open an email service, or send a message. If you deliberately select and copy text, Android handles the copied text through its clipboard. Clipboard contents are outside the app’s private game storage and are governed by Android and any apps you choose to paste into.

4. Network requests and external services

All 60 included hidden-object levels, artwork, fonts, and audio are bundled with the app. Daily Challenges are generated on your device using its local date; they are not fetched from a server. No remote puzzle-pack server is configured in this release, and its download form is unavailable. Starting the app, playing the included game, and reading the in-app privacy policy do not require network requests. The app does not send gameplay telemetry to the developer.

The app retains optional puzzle-pack support for possible future releases. If a future release enables downloads, its policy and store disclosures must explain the server and its data handling before distribution. A download would require your confirmation before contacting the configured HTTPS publisher. The server would receive normal request information, including your IP address, request headers, and the requested pack address. Puzzle packs are validated puzzle descriptions, not executable code or remote image trackers.

Google Play, Android, and your email provider may process information under their own settings and privacy policies. Their platform services are separate from the app’s local game storage. Email service providers may process information in countries other than yours; their storage locations, retention, and privacy controls are governed by those providers, not by the game.

5. Android permissions and backups

The Android release declares the permissions listed below. Internet and vibration are Android normal permissions and do not require a dangerous-permission approval dialog. The app-specific receiver permission is signature-protected. These declarations do not give the developer access to your local game saves.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos/personal-file access, notification, advertising-ID, or Android AdServices permissions. Music and sound effects play bundled audio; the app does not record audio. Its private local database and preferences do not require permission to browse your personal files.

Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup of the app’s game data and preferences are disabled in the Android configuration. The developer does not provide a cloud backup or recovery service. The Android text-processing intent query only supports system text-selection actions; it is not a permission to collect your installed-app list or transmit selected text automatically.

  • android.permission.INTERNET: retained for optional HTTPS puzzle-pack capabilities. No pack publisher is configured and no gameplay data is sent in this release.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: used for optional haptic feedback when you find an object; you can turn vibration off in Settings.
  • DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: an app-specific, signature-protected permission added by Android libraries to protect internal broadcast receivers. It does not grant access to personal data.

6. How data is protected

Local saves use the app’s private Android storage, which Android normally separates from other apps. The app does not expose saves through a public game server or export them automatically. Device security, such as a screen lock, operating-system updates, and avoiding unauthorized device access, helps protect those local records. The app does not provide a separate encrypted vault and does not claim that its SQLite records are individually encrypted by the app.

Support emails are handled through the email services you and the developer use; their access controls and transport protections apply. Access to the developer’s support correspondence is limited to what is needed to handle support and legal obligations. Do not send sensitive information in a support message. No device storage, email system, or Internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. How long data is kept

Local gameplay records remain on your device until you reset progress, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Reset progress keeps your settings, the local started-playing flag, compatibility preferences, and installed packs; those remain until you clear all app data or uninstall. A fresh starting coin balance and today’s new Daily Challenge selection are created after a reset. The developer has no remote copy of these local saves.

Support correspondence is kept only for as long as necessary to respond to your request, resolve the issue, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request its deletion using the contact below. If information must be retained for a legal obligation, only the necessary information is retained for that purpose and for the required period.

8. Your choices and deletion

Because the app does not create accounts or upload gameplay records, there is no app account to delete and the developer does not hold a copy of your local saves to recover or delete remotely. Privacy setup or consent to tracking is not required to play this offline, ad-free release.

  • Change sound, music, vibration, volume, and gameplay pace in Settings.
  • Use Settings, then Reset progress, to delete saved gameplay history, hints, daily history, completion receipts, and earned coins after confirmation. You restart with the initial game coins. Settings, compatibility preferences, the started-playing flag, and installed packs are kept. This is a logical deletion of gameplay records, not a separate forensic secure-erasure feature.
  • To remove all local app data, use Android’s app-storage clearing option or uninstall the app. Local progress cannot be recovered through the developer. Removing app data does not delete emails you already sent or text you copied to the system clipboard; manage those through your email service and Android.
  • Contact the developer to request access, a copy, correction, or deletion of information you sent in support correspondence, subject to applicable law and any necessary legal retention. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Children’s privacy

The app does not request a child’s age, birthday, contact details, or an account, and has no ads, tracking, purchases, public profiles, or chat. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through gameplay. Parents or guardians should supervise web browsing and any support communication. If you believe a child’s personal information was sent in a support message, please contact the developer so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Any future collection, tracking, advertising, or remote-content changes must be disclosed accurately before the changed app is distributed, with any required permissions and consent obtained before the new data handling begins.
